## The pain

Before a marketing agency can start working with a client, the client has to grant it a bunch of access. Without a central place, the instructions and the tracking of what’s already been granted end up scattered. And the requests change over the course of the partnership.

## The idea

A checklist shared via link that keeps the instructions and the tracking of each access in one place.

- The client opens the link and that’s it: no sign-up required.
- The agency can see what’s already been granted.
- Requests can be updated as the partnership goes on.

## Mission status

In development, with no public version or demo yet. When there is one, this page gets the links.
