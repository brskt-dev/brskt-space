## The problem

Before a marketing agency can start working with a client, it needs that client to grant access. Without a central place, the instructions for each access request and the tracking of what's already been granted end up scattered. And the requests change over the course of the partnership.

## How it works

Conta Comigo puts the instructions and the tracking in one place: a checklist shared via link. The client accesses it through the link, with no sign-up required. Requests can be updated as the partnership goes on.
